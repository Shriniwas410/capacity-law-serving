Why speculation does not speed up a 235B MoE on 32 GB RAM

Qwen3-235B-A22B is 133 GB in 4-bit GGUF. This page is the capacity law from the traces: a draft is only cheap to verify if the union of experts fits in the expert cache. Drag the slot budget. Numbers come from the public measurements/*.json files, not from a demo.

GitHub repo · paper PDF · RESULTS.md

The table does not fit. A 12-slot slab does.

Feasibility is expert-table bytes versus RAM, not “235 billion parameters.” Greedy decode still streams 94 layers × top-8 × 10.85 MB ≈ 8 GB of experts per token before cache hits. Full CPU mmap dies allocating a 105 GB CPU_REPACK buffer.

Bar length is log-scaled so 12 GB and 133 GB can share an axis. 12-slot greedy decode is 0.38 tok/s at 16.70 GiB RSS (ab235b_12s_verdict.json). O_DIRECT QD8 on this VHDX is 2.34–2.37 GB/s.

Slot budget versus unique-expert demand U(k)

Each token activates 8 of 128 experts. A k-token verify is one I/O generation only if the unique experts in that window, U(k), fit in s slots. Mean U(4) = 18.98 — sharing is real (about 0.59× a naive 32), but the union still exceeds 16.

Per-layer mean T is not what the engine admits

HorizonSpec T is a per-layer mean on target-oracle routing (uk_umax_235b.json). The engine takes the minimum admitted length over 94 layers (_min_layer_T.json). Not tok/s. Engine min at 24 slots was not timed on this 21 Gi WSL box.

High α, wrong verify granularity

A 0.6B same-family draft is accepted at α = 0.75–0.79 on a 16-token smoke. That is not a wall-clock win. Stock llama.cpp verify with -ub 1 slows the 12-slot run. Serial expert waves make multi-token verify legal without 24 resident slots (3 × top-8). Batched verify (-ub 8) returns to parity, not 1.5×. Local --moe-stream-umax v1 collapsed α to 0.

12-slot cache, CPU-only, n=16, Fibonacci prompt. -ub 1 · -ub 8 · umax v1. Do not mix with the 0.44 tok/s GPU-split llama-server figure from a different stack.

What this changes, and what it does not

The traces tell the next paper what to build: prefix FFN on rows 0–1 (serial waves if U(2)>12), mask the suffix by token row, and do not skip row-0 logits. A global U≤12 gate repeats the umax v1 loss (p(T≥2)=0.12% at 12 slots). Causal lag-2 prefetch recovered 0% of the LRU–OPT gap on these traces.

This is not interactive chat, not a GPU result, and not a claim that HorizonSpec already works in the engine. It is a map of the wall so “just speculate” stops being the default move for SSD MoE on a RAM slab that does not hold the table.
